## The game (one sentence)
A co-op phone dungeon crawl for 2–5 adults: one host runs the table, everyone else plays heroes through a short campaign under a normal street — dry humor, readable card fights, clear win/loss.

## Why it’s good (not a kit)
- **A campaign, not a sample:** 8 chapters. Chapter 1 free. Rest unlock as a pack later.
- **A night has a shape:** ~60 min → boss → win or wipe → recap worth screenshots.
- **Combat is the sport:** Might & Magic–style cards (energy, creatures, End turn) — deepen, don’t dilute with trivia modes.
- **Host looks competent:** GM-light pacing, no rules lawyer, phones only.
- **Tone for 41:** competent, dry, a little weird. No sticky-toffee kids’ book voice. No “host kit” marketing.

## Setting
**Below Maple Street.** A quiet town. Under one ordinary house: a mapped underworld of service tunnels, sealed wards, and things that learned the floor plan. The party aren’t chosen ones — they’re the people who showed up.

## Player fantasy
Be the friend who runs a tight fantasy night that actually finishes. Heroes feel distinct. Bosses feel unfair until you learn them. Loot matters next chapter.

## Core loop (one chapter)
1. Host opens session → share code  
2. Players pick/confirm hero (4 roles)  
3. 5 rooms: explore beat → choice or check → **or** card combat  
4. Boss room (combat, fail = wipe with recap, not soft-lock)  
5. End: loot + chapter stamp → party state saved for next chapter (local/mock unlock OK until paid)

## Card combat (rules spine — keep Pass 2 shape)
- Shared energy per turn; play ally/action cards; End turn resolves hits
- Enemy intent readable before you commit
- Wipe = chapter fail + recap; victory = loot card into party stash
- Deepen: 1 boss pattern per chapter (telegraph → punish → punish)

## Campaign map (8 chapters)
1. **The Hatch** (free) — find the way down; boss: **The Concierge** (door that charges a toll in HP)
2. **Meter Room** — light and power; boss: **Brownout**
3. **Lost Laundry** — wrong memories; boss: **The Cycle**
4. **Union Stack** — old labor wards; boss: **Shop Steward**
5. **Flood Gate** — rising water timing; boss: **Surge**
6. **Archive** — names on the walls; boss: **Indexer**
7. **Boiler Choir** — heat and noise; boss: **Pressure**
8. **Street Level** — climb out changed; boss: **Maple Itself**

Chapter 1 ships complete in Pass 4. Chapters 2–8: titles + boss name + one-line hook in data (locked stubs OK).

## Chapter 1 beat sheet — The Hatch
1. **Porch light** — choice: force the hatch / talk it open / find the spare key (fail-forward always advances)
2. **First ladder** — skill check (Keen shines); trap telegraph
3. **Toll booth** — roleplay/choice; sets Concierge rules
4. **Side tunnel** — first full card fight (small pack)
5. **The Concierge** — boss; energy tax mechanic; kill or wipe

Loot on clear: **Spare Key** (party stash — used in Ch.2 stub).

## Voice
Short. Dry. Specific. No “Money Brief.” No “credible page this week.” Room text under ~40 words. Banter optional one-liners, not essays.
